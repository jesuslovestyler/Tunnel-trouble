How to play

  • Dig with the arrow pad — you carve tunnels as you go, slower through solid dirt than open tunnel.
  • PUMP shoots your air hose the way you're facing. If it latches a critter, keep tapping PUMP — three puffs pops it! Stop tapping and it deflates and wriggles free.
  • Burrow Bugs drift through dirt and chase you in tunnels. Ember Imps stop and breathe flame down open tunnels — stay out of the line of fire.
  • Boulders: dig the dirt out from under one and it wobbles, then falls — crushing anything underneath, including you. +250 per critter squashed.
  • Deeper critters are worth more points. Pop a few and a bonus carrot appears in the middle tunnel — grab it before it vanishes.
  • Clear every critter to reach the next level. You have 3 lives — don't get touched!

On a keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to dig, Space to pump.
